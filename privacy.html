Privacy policy

Minty: Pill & Care Reminder. Little nudges, big care.

Last updated: [date of release] · Hosted at: https://mintycare.vercel.app/privacy.html

Who is responsible

Minty is made by RGTECH ([postal address, if required in your country]). RGTECH is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. For any question or data request, write to coded9782@gmail.com.

Minty is a reminder and logging tool for regimens you enter. It does not diagnose, treat, recommend, calculate or change doses. It is not a medical device.

What Minty keeps

Minty keeps everything you enter in the app:

  • medications, schedules and the doses you log
  • checkups and appointments
  • refills and stock counts
  • the activity feed of what happened in the app
  • the optional cycle tracker (off by default): period days, flow, mood, symptoms and notes
  • your settings

This is health data. Minty stores it only on your device, in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. On Android the database is encrypted with SQLCipher using a key held by the device; if encryption cannot be turned on for a device, Settings > About says so and the data stays protected by Android's file encryption. Android automatic backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Minty.

If you turn on the optional app lock, Minty uses your phone's own screen lock, fingerprint or face unlock through the Android system prompt. Minty never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. It only learns from the system whether the unlock succeeded.

The cycle tracker works out estimates (next period, fertile window, patterns) on your device from your own entries. Nothing about your cycle is ever synced, shown in notifications or widgets, or included in exports unless you tick it yourself.

Scanning a medicine label (optional)

If you scan a box or label, Minty uses your camera or a photo you pick. The text is read on your device; the photo is never uploaded and is not kept after reading. Only if you tap Check online, Minty sends a few words from the label (no photo and nothing about you) to the free RxNorm service of the U.S. National Library of Medicine to look up the medicine name.

How long it is kept

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. The activity feed keeps one year of entries; older entries are deleted automatically. Uninstalling Minty, or using Delete all data, removes everything.

No analytics, no tracking

Minty has no account and no server. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party crash reporting. Nothing is sent to the developer.

If Minty stops working, you can export a local crash log yourself and choose whether to share it.

Google Calendar (optional)

Google Calendar sync is off unless you turn it on. If you turn it on, Minty sends your checkups to a calendar named "Minty" in your own Google account, and reads changes back.

  • By default it sends only the word "Appointment" and the time. Real titles and places are sent only if you choose that.
  • Medication data, notes and cycle data are never sent.
  • If you choose "Import existing appointments", Minty reads your primary calendar for the next 12 months and copies only the events you pick.
  • Minty keeps your Google account email on the device, to show which account is connected. It never stores your Google password or long-lived tokens.
  • Data travels only between your device and Google, encrypted with HTTPS.

Minty's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to show and keep your checkups in sync on your device. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never read by people.

You can disconnect at any time in Settings > Calendar sync. Disconnecting revokes Minty's access. You can also choose to delete the Minty calendar from Google. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com.

Your device's calendar (optional)

If you choose to add checkups to a calendar on this device, Minty writes those events there. That calendar may be synced by its own provider (for example your Google or Samsung account) under that provider's terms. You can remove the events Minty added at any time.

Files you share

Reports, data exports and backups leave Minty only when you share or save them yourself, through the Android share sheet or the system file picker. Before you share, Minty warns you that the file contains health data. The copies made for sharing are deleted from Minty's private cache afterwards. Anyone who receives a file can read it, so share it only with people you trust.

Backups are your responsibility

Minty does not back up your data anywhere. If you lose or reset your phone without a backup file, your data cannot be recovered. A manual backup file is encrypted with a password you choose. That password cannot be recovered. Keep backup files somewhere private: they contain health data.

Minimum age

Minty is for people aged 16 or older, or the age of digital consent where you live if that is higher. You confirm this when you set up the app.

Your choices and rights

You can see, change and export your data in the app at any time. Because the data never leaves your device, the developer cannot see, correct or delete it for you.

Depending on where you live, data protection law may give you rights over your personal data. In the EU and UK this is the GDPR. Those rights include access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. Minty lets you exercise them directly in the app:

  • export your data (Reports > Export data);
  • edit or delete entries;
  • Delete all data.

For questions about privacy or this policy, write to coded9782@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

How to delete everything

Go to Settings, then Delete all data, and type DELETE. Minty then does all of this:

  • cancels every reminder and alarm;
  • disconnects Google, and can delete the Minty calendar there;
  • can remove the events it added to your phone's calendar;
  • clears its home screen widgets;
  • erases all your data on this device;
  • starts again from the beginning.

If the app lock is on and you cannot unlock, the lock screen offers "Erase Minty and start over", which does the same without showing any data. Backup files you saved earlier are not touched.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version ships with an app update and is shown in the app with its date. The hosted copy at https://mintycare.vercel.app/privacy.html is updated at the same time.
