Today

Never wonder “did I take it?” again.

Why you need it: when the days blur together, it’s easy to take a dose twice or miss it completely. Minty shows what’s due right now and what’s already done — no guessing.

  • Reminders ring even when your phone is asleep
  • Taken, Snooze, Skip, or log the real time you took it

Packs, patches & rings

Always know which pill you’re on.

Why you need it: with 21-day or 84-day packs, weekly patches and monthly rings, losing count happens — and it matters. Minty counts for you, including break and placebo weeks.

  • “Pill 16 of 21” at a glance — 84-day packs too
  • Knows your break week and when the next pack starts
  • Patch, ring and injection-day trackers too
Calendar

Your whole month, in one look.

Why you need it: memory isn’t reliable. A calendar that marks each day as taken, skipped or missed shows patterns you’d never notice — like always forgetting on weekends.

  • Day, week and month views
  • Clear marks for taken, skipped and missed
  • Checkups show up on the same calendar
History

Progress you can actually see.

Why you need it: sticking to a routine is easier when you can see it working. Streaks and simple percentages show your effort honestly — no guilt trips, just facts.

  • Current and best streak
  • How many doses were taken, and how many on time
  • A breakdown for every medication
Doctor report

Walk into appointments prepared.

Why you need it: “How have you been taking your medicine?” is hard to answer from memory. Hand your doctor a clean PDF instead — every dose, every skip and the reason why.

Checkups

Visits, tests and follow-ups, remembered.

Why you need it: a lab test booked three weeks ahead is easy to forget, and a missed follow-up can mean waiting weeks for another slot. Minty reminds you before it’s time.

  • Past visits stay listed for reference

Questions
When can I get it?

Minty is coming to Google Play soon. Check back here — the button above will take you straight to it.

What about iPhone?

Not yet. Minty is Android-only for now; an iPhone version is planned.

Does Minty give medical advice?

No. Minty reminds you and keeps a log of the plan you and your doctor already have. It never suggests, calculates or changes a dose, and it isn’t a medical device.

Is my cycle data private?

Yes. The cycle tracker is off until you turn it on. Your entries stay on your phone, are never synced, and never appear in notifications or widgets. Predictions are estimates from your own log: they are not birth control and not medical advice.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Your data lives only on the phone, so make a backup from Settings now and then. The backup file is encrypted with a password you choose.
